# Software and apps used by Hungarian pigeon fanciers (postagalamb / galambász), as of Nov 2026

Research note: about 18 web searches and fetches, 2026-11-18. Hungarian-language searches on general engines return very little for this niche: most Hungarian galambász software has almost no web footprint, and nothing was found on Facebook groups or forums. Several key sites could not be fetched: galambom.hu (TLS certificate error), szoftverbox.hu (connection refused), benzinghungary.hu (certificate expired) and archiv.postagalamb.hu (DNS failure). Claims that rest only on search-engine snippets are marked "(snippet)".

## 1. Which loft, pedigree, results and auction programs are used in Hungary?

### Takeaway
Hungary has no single dominant phone app for loft or pedigree records. The landscape has four parts:
- **Federation results platform.** The MPSZ (Magyar Postagalamb Sportszövetség) runs galambom.hu, a free web system for results, members and "my pigeons", with no pedigree function.
- **Clock-maker ecosystems.** Benzing (MyPigeons), Bricon (exclusive Hungarian distributor since 2019), plus Tauris and Tipes clocks, whose data is imported into galambom.hu.
- **One Hungarian-made pedigree, loft and auction web service.** pigeon.plus (formerly "pped", pigeonped.com, made by Ceberit, Hungarian sole trader). It is the closest Hungarian equivalent to Porumbar.
- **Older and international tools.** An old Hungarian Windows program, Szoftverbox "Galamb-ász", plus international tools with a Hungarian UI: Pigeon Planner (100% Hungarian translation) and PigeonDB (Magyar listed).

### Cited findings

**A. galambom.hu, the official results and registry system of MPSZ (Hungary)**
- Launched in autumn 2015 by the Magyar Postagalamb Sportszövetség. The source quote: "2015 őszén elindult a galambom.hu internetes címen található rendszerünk". It is an online system for registering racing pigeons and fanciers and for calculating and publishing results. Article dated 2016-04-25, by secretary general Pethő Attila — [postagalambsport.hu](https://postagalambsport.hu/2016/04/25/eredmenyszamolas-megujulva-galambom-hu/)
- Features: a "Galambjaim" menu where members manage their own pigeons after linking their account to their membership record; official ("Hivatalos") and custom ("Egyedi") results, with statistics and maps; distances, release points, basic entries (alapnevezés) and membership data. Lost-pigeon matching emails both the finder and the owner. Usable 24/7 from computer, tablet or phone. Registration is optional — [postagalambsport.hu](https://postagalambsport.hu/2016/04/25/eredmenyszamolas-megujulva-galambom-hu/)
- Clocks: the system "a legtöbb elektronikus óra alapnevezési adatait tudja fogadni" (accepts basic-entry data from most electronic clocks). For traditional clocks, results are entered by hand or imported from a spreadsheet. The article mentions no pedigree or breeding records and no price — [postagalambsport.hu](https://postagalambsport.hu/2016/04/25/eredmenyszamolas-megujulva-galambom-hu/)
- An older MPSZ page names the electronic clocks whose databases can be imported: Benzing, Bricon, Tauris and Tipes. Club administrators can also enter or correct ring numbers by hand (snippet, page about 10 years old) — [archiv.postagalamb.hu](http://archiv.postagalamb.hu/?versenyek%2FGyujtes_es_bontas_elektromos_rogzitokkel=)
- MPSZ also handles ring orders through paper or PDF forms: the 2026 ring-order form and the association and individual data sheets are on the "Nyomtatványok" page (snippet) — [postagalambsport.hu/nyomtatvanyok](https://postagalambsport.hu/nyomtatvanyok/). The 2025 ring-order deadline was 30 June 2024 — [postagalambsport.hu](https://postagalambsport.hu/2024/05/08/gyururendeles-a-2025-os-evre-hataridok/)

**B. Benzing / MyPigeons (Germany), clocks plus an online results platform**
- MyPigeons (mypigeons.benzing.live) has a Hungarian-language interface, a language selector with about 20 other languages, and a footer reading "2013–2026". Its public tools are release sites and lost pigeons. The page mentions no pedigree or loft-management features — [mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/hu/results](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/hu/results/)
- MPSZ results have been published there, for example "Sport B (300–600 km), Magyar Postagalamb Sportszövetség, 2017" — [MyPigeons](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/hu/results/2017/c-12749-category-b-middle-distance/o-50-magyar-postagalamb-sportszovetseg/)
- MyPigeons has a Hungarian-language member-registry guide in its Slovakia section, which suggests Hungarian-speaking Slovak clubs use it (snippet) — [MyPigeons SK/HU guide](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/slovakia/hu/guide/database-administration/)
- A Hungarian derby notes that Benzing clocks need a D00 file per fancier plus a PIB file with all data, while other clocks rarely cause problems (snippet) — [galambderby.hu](https://galambderby.hu/)
- There is a Hungarian Benzing distributor site ("Live rendszer" page), but its TLS certificate had expired and it could not be read — [benzinghungary.hu](http://www.benzinghungary.hu/live-rendszer.html)

**C. Bricon (Belgium)**
- Herold Tamás has been the exclusive Hungarian distributor of Bricon products since July 2019. The site shows a new Bricon X-treme clock that can send email and works with the "Print Manager" program (snippet) — [bricon.hu](http://www.bricon.hu/)

**D. Tauris, Unikon and Tipes clocks**
- Tauris and Tipes clock data is accepted by the MPSZ system (snippet, old page) — [archiv.postagalamb.hu](http://archiv.postagalamb.hu/?versenyek%2FGyujtes_es_bontas_elektromos_rogzitokkel=)
- Used Tauris clocks and complete Tauris sets are advertised on the Hungarian classifieds portal "Versenygalamb Portál" (snippet) — [versenygalambportal.gportal.hu](http://versenygalambportal.gportal.hu/gindex.php?pg=19255993&sid=0&offset=0&replyid=25997449)
- No Hungarian distributor was found for Tauris or Unikon.

**E. pigeon.plus (formerly "pped" / pigeonped.com), Hungarian, web**
- Footer: "Made by Ceberit". It carries a Hungarian sole-trader registration (EV-56448665), a +36 phone number and a copyright range of 2013–2026. The site recently moved to a new server and platform and was renamed from pped to pplus; the old address still works — [pigeonped.com/hu](https://pigeonped.com/hu/)
- Features:
  - **Galambjaim:** pigeon records with photos and a family tree. AutoFill fills in ancestors by searching the records of "several hundred breeders" (a shared database).
  - **Pedigree:** several styles, loft logo, printing, save as image or PDF, and an option to highlight inbreeding.
  - **Dúc menedzser (loft manager):** virtual loft, pairs placed in nests, chicks tracked from laying to hatching to ringing, and foster parents recorded.
  - **Auctions:** developed with support from FCI Grand Prix VÁC; the first auction offers birds from the test-site races.

  Source: [pigeonped.com/hu](https://pigeonped.com/hu/) and [download page](https://www.pigeonped.com/hu/26-letoltes)
- Language: currently Hungarian only; English is "in development" — [pigeonped.com/hu](https://pigeonped.com/hu/)
- Public pedigree pages exist, for example [pigeonped.com/hu/pigeon-data?view=pigeon&uid=375&pid=17973](https://pigeonped.com/hu/pigeon-data?view=pigeon&uid=375&pid=17973)
- No prices, user numbers, or Android/iOS apps are shown on the pages fetched — [pigeonped.com/hu](https://pigeonped.com/hu/)

**F. Szoftverbox "Galamb-ász" / "Galambász program", Hungarian Windows desktop (old)**
- A paid pigeon-records program that identifies birds by ring number and stores parents, origin, pedigree sheet (4 generations), purchase, hatch and sale dates. A demo can be downloaded; the full version is listed at 10,000 Ft + ÁFA (snippet only; the site refused the connection, and the version date is unknown) — [szoftverbox.hu](https://szoftverbox.hu/)

**G. International tools with a Hungarian user interface**
- **Pigeon Planner** (Belgium, free and open source, desktop): the Hungarian translation on Launchpad is 100% complete, last changed 2025-02-27 (seen on a qastaging mirror) — [Launchpad translations](https://translations.qastaging.launchpad.net/pigeonplanner/+translations). Pigeon Planner can also make automatic backups — [Softpedia](https://www.softpedia.com/get/Others/Miscellaneous/Pigeon-Planner.shtml)
- **PigeonDB** (AllBreed Solutions, web, any browser):
  - 16 UI languages including Magyar and Română.
  - Printable 4 and 5 generation pedigrees with optional QR codes, an interactive chart up to 10 generations, and inbreeding coefficient (COI) for a pigeon and for hypothetical pairings.
  - Race and training-toss log with speed.
  - Free trial, then a paid plan; no EUR price shown.

  Source: [pigeondb.com](https://pigeondb.com/)
- **AviRings Pedigree** (web): the login page offers Hungarian (snippet) and it can import from other pedigree software — [pedigree.avirings.com](https://pedigree.avirings.com/Login), [how to register](https://pedigree.avirings.com/NewsAndUpdates/how%20to%20register). AviRings also runs a "Derby PRO 2026" — [derby.avirings.com](https://derby.avirings.com/en/Page/SeasonInfo)
- **MyPigeon App** (iOS): free with in-app purchases and 5-generation pedigrees. A search snippet said its reviews come from Hungarian breeders, but this could not be verified (App Store returned HTTP 429) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mypigeon-app/id1117490387)
- **Compuclub** (Netherlands, Benzing family) offers the Windows loft program "Compustam" for Windows 7, 8 and 10 (Australia page) — [compuclub.nl](https://www.compuclub.nl/australia/compustam.html)

**H. Comparable apps and prices nearby (for benchmarking)**
- **myloft.ro** (Romania): multilingual mobile app, web subscription €35 per year, 15-day free trial — [myloft.ro](https://myloft.ro/?lang=en)
- **pigeonroots:** from €59 per year, free basic tier up to 60 active pigeons — [Slashdot](https://slashdot.org/software/p/pigeonroots/)
- **PigeonPro: Loft & Pigeon Manager** (iOS): free with in-app purchases and multi-generation pedigrees; Hungarian is not among its listed languages — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6743889133), [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/pigeonpro-loft-manager/6771820214)

### Inferences
- For racing data, Hungarian fanciers live in the federation and clock ecosystem: galambom.hu plus their clock's own software. For pedigrees, the visible Hungarian-made option is pigeon.plus. Its "AutoFill from several hundred breeders" points to a modest but real user base, likely in the hundreds of lofts rather than thousands.
- Unlike Porumbar, galambom.hu holds the fancier's ring list in the federation system. A Hungarian product could add value by importing it, but no public API was found.

### Gaps
- No Google Play listing with Hungarian UI or Hungarian reviews was found. Install counts for any galambász app are unknown.
- No prices were found for pigeon.plus, PigeonDB, Benzing/Compuclub software or Bricon Print Manager in Hungary.
- The second Hungarian body (for fancy or other pigeons) and its tools were not identified. One hobby site says "two associations" issue rings, but it was not verified — [szolnoki-bagdetta.hupont.hu](http://szolnoki-bagdetta.hupont.hu/24/galambtartastenyesztes)
- No evidence was found on use by Hungarian-speaking fanciers in Transylvania or Serbia. For Slovakia there is only the MyPigeons Hungarian-language guide.
- Not checked: Pipa-related tools, PigeonHub, Winpigeon/WinGlobal, Pigeon Organizer and Loft Manager in a Hungarian context. No Hungarian-context sources surfaced for them.

## 2. Features that Hungarian and international tools have and a simple phone pedigree app may lack

### Takeaway
Compared with Porumbar, these tools are stronger on four things, mostly provided by the federation, clock and PigeonDB layers:
- integration with race results and clocks (galambom.hu, Benzing MyPigeons, Bricon);
- a shared ancestor database with auto-fill (pigeon.plus);
- inbreeding marking or coefficient (pigeon.plus, PigeonDB);
- a nest and chick lifecycle, plus auctions (pigeon.plus).

They are weaker on phone-first simplicity for elderly users, on AI import of pedigree photos, and on visual extras such as fan view or relatives graph. No source found offers AI photo import.

### Cited findings
- **Clock data import:** Benzing, Bricon, Tauris and Tipes, with manual ring-number correction (snippet, old) — [archiv.postagalamb.hu](http://archiv.postagalamb.hu/?versenyek%2FGyujtes_es_bontas_elektromos_rogzitokkel=). For traditional clocks, Excel or manual entry — [postagalambsport.hu](https://postagalambsport.hu/2016/04/25/eredmenyszamolas-megujulva-galambom-hu/)
- **Results, statistics and maps, lost-pigeon email matching:** [postagalambsport.hu](https://postagalambsport.hu/2016/04/25/eredmenyszamolas-megujulva-galambom-hu/)
- **AutoFill of ancestors** from other breeders' records, **inbreeding highlighting** on the pedigree, **loft logo** on the pedigree, and a **nest, egg, hatch and ring** workflow with foster parents — [pigeonped.com/hu](https://pigeonped.com/hu/)
- **Auctions** with FCI Grand Prix VÁC — [pigeonped.com/hu](https://pigeonped.com/hu/)
- **COI for planned pairings, a 10-generation interactive chart, QR on printed pedigrees, and race and training logs** — [pigeondb.com](https://pigeondb.com/)
- **Ring orders** are handled by federation PDF forms, not inside apps — [postagalambsport.hu/nyomtatvanyok](https://postagalambsport.hu/nyomtatvanyok/)

### Inferences
- **Where Porumbar is roughly on par:**
  - Pedigree printing with QR code and a public share link (as do PigeonDB and the pigeon.plus public pedigree pages).
  - Pair planning (as does pigeon.plus).
  - A sales list (roughly comparable to pigeon.plus auctions, though less structured).
- **What Porumbar lacks:**
  - Inbreeding coefficient on a planned pairing.
  - Nest, egg and hatch tracking.
  - A shared cross-breeder auto-fill database.
  - Race results or clock import.
- **What appears unique to Porumbar:** AI photo-to-pedigree import, an elderly-optimised UI, and the value-coloured dashboard and relatives graph. No Hungarian tool advertises these.
- **Price:** Porumbar at about €30 per year is in line with myloft.ro (€35 per year) and below pigeonroots (€59 per year). The old Hungarian desktop Galamb-ász was listed at 10,000 Ft + ÁFA one-off (snippet).

### Gaps
- No source confirmed eye photos, health or vaccination records, or Excel export in any Hungarian tool.

## 3. What users complain about

### Takeaway
No first-hand Hungarian user complaints were found. Searches returned no forum or Facebook text, and the App Store page was rate-limited. Only structural weaknesses visible from the vendors' own pages can be noted.

### Cited findings
- **pigeon.plus:** Hungarian only, with English "in development"; no mobile app is mentioned; no public price — [pigeonped.com/hu](https://pigeonped.com/hu/)
- **Galamb-ász:** a Windows desktop program with a 4-generation pedigree and a demo-and-licence model (snippet) — [szoftverbox.hu](https://szoftverbox.hu/)
- **galambom.hu:** no pedigree or breeding function is mentioned — [postagalambsport.hu](https://postagalambsport.hu/2016/04/25/eredmenyszamolas-megujulva-galambom-hu/)
- **Benzing clocks:** Benzing needs special D00 and PIB files for some organisers (snippet) — [galambderby.hu](https://galambderby.hu/)
- **Site upkeep:** several Hungarian galambász sites show weak maintenance. benzinghungary.hu had an expired TLS certificate, the galambom.hu certificate did not match its host, archiv.postagalamb.hu was not resolvable, and szoftverbox.hu refused connections. These are observations made during this research on 2026-11-18, not published sources.

### Inferences
- The general complaints (old Windows UI, no phone version, poor fit for older fanciers) are plausible, given that the Hungarian-made options are a desktop program and a desktop-style website. They are not backed by quoted user reviews.

### Gaps
- No user reviews, ratings or forum threads were found. Hungarian Facebook groups (for example postagalamb or galambász groups) are not indexed by the search tools used.

## 4. Hungarian online databases and result sites that apps integrate with

### Takeaway
The central Hungarian results database is galambom.hu (MPSZ, since 2015). Benzing MyPigeons also hosts some MPSZ results (for example 2017). The federation site postagalambsport.hu (also postagalamb.hu) publishes news, forms and PDF best-pigeon lists. No evidence was found of a public API, or of any pedigree app pulling from galambom.hu.

### Cited findings
- **galambom.hu:** official and custom results, member pigeons, release points, lost pigeons — [postagalambsport.hu](https://postagalambsport.hu/2016/04/25/eredmenyszamolas-megujulva-galambom-hu/)
- **MPSZ results on MyPigeons (2017)** — [MyPigeons](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/hu/results/2017/c-12749-category-b-middle-distance/o-50-magyar-postagalamb-sportszovetseg/)
- **MPSZ "World's best pigeon" lists** are published as PDF with ring numbers (January 2026 issue) — [postagalambsport.hu PDF](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/PGS-januar_honlapra.pdf); the federation home page is [postagalamb.hu](https://www.postagalamb.hu/)
- **Derby races:** Royal Derby, paid-prize races, with its own clock-file requirements — [galambderby.hu](https://galambderby.hu/); FCI Grand Prix VÁC, linked to pigeon.plus auctions — [pigeonped.com/hu](https://pigeonped.com/hu/)
- **Hungarian classifieds portal** for clocks and pigeons — [Versenygalamb Portál](http://versenygalambportal.gportal.hu/gindex.php?pg=19255993&offset=40)

### Inferences
- A Hungarian version of Porumbar could differentiate itself by letting users paste or import their galambom.hu "Galambjaim" ring list and results (if exportable), and by printing pedigrees that show results from galambom.hu.

### Gaps
- Whether galambom.hu offers an export or API is unknown, because the site could not be fetched.
- The current user count of galambom.hu or MPSZ membership was not found.
