# Hungarian pigeon racing federation (MPSZ), rings and rules relevant to a pedigree app

Research date: 2026-11-18. Primary sources are the federation's own site (postagalambsport.hu), its regulations (PDFs downloaded and read in full text), its forms, its monthly magazine (Sept 2026) and the Benzing-hosted results portal. Name note: the official name is **Magyar Postagalamb Sportszövetség** (the site abbreviates it "MPS"; press and fanciers use "MPSZ"), not "Magyar Postagalambsport Szövetség". Founded 1882. Office: 1076 Budapest, Verseny u. 14. Old domain postagalamb.hu (email info@postagalamb.hu); mpsz.hu is a parked "coming soon" domain, not the federation.

## 1. MPSZ structure: regions, clubs, membership and its trend, age profile

### Takeaway
MPSZ is a three-level federation: national federation → "versenyszervezők" (race organisers, also called tagszövetség / versenykerület / klub, each with a two-digit number such as 10 Hajdú, 23 Karancs, 27 Szabolcs) → clubs (egyesület or "civil társaság", coded like E-01). Membership is in the low thousands and shrinking: ~5,500 (older, undated figure) → 3,987 (around the 2022 140th anniversary), and the federation's own magazine in 2026 says membership "keeps falling". No age data found.

### Cited Findings
- Official name "Magyar Postagalamb Sportszövetség", founded 1882 ("Alapítva 1882-ben"); site has news to November 2026 — [postagalambsport.hu](https://postagalambsport.hu/)
- Address 1076 Budapest, Verseny u. 14 (board minutes header, Jan 2024) — [Elnökségi jegyzőkönyv 2024.01.06](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/elnoksegi-jkv.-2024.01.06.-Budapest-FMK-kibovitett-elnoksegi-ules.pdf)
- Hierarchy (Registration and Transfer Rules, 2020): the federation keeps a register ("Nyilvántartott Tagság") of its legal-person members and all their members, multi-level, down to natural persons; every registered member (legal entity, civil társaság, natural person) gets a **registration number** ("nyilvántartási számot kapnak"). A club / civil társaság may belong to only one race organiser ("versenyszervező"). Transfer window for fanciers between clubs: 15 Oct – 15 Nov, for the following season; transferred fanciers must race 2 years with the new organiser. Signed by president Hegyi György, 17 Oct 2020 — [Nyilvántartási és átigazolási szabályzat 2020](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/MPS-Vegleges-atigazolasi-szabalyzat-uj-2020-10-21.pdf)
- Race rules (OSVSZ 2023) name the organising units as "Tagszövetség, Versenykerület, Klub és a Sportszövetség"; a club may race only within one versenykerület/tagszövetség; a fancier may race for only one club within a versenykerület/tagszövetség; dual/multiple membership is possible only across different organisers, and only one "No. 1 team" (and thus National Championship scoring) per fancier — [Országos Sport és Versenyszabályzat 2023.01.27, §5](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- To race, a fancier must be a properly registered member, pay the federation fee, buy an annual **race licence (versenyengedély)**, and give a written GDPR consent to the federation's data-processing policy; licence fee is per season, per fancier, scaled by the number of pigeons in the base entry; licence paid via organisers by 15 April (old birds) / 31 August (young birds) — [OSVSZ 2023, §5 1/–3/](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Organiser codes visible in data: ring-order summary sheet for district "23 Karancs" lists its clubs as E-01, E-03, E-05 … E-14, V-09, Z-35, with a column for each club's current headcount ("jelenlegi létszáma") — [Gyűrűrendelés 2027 versenyszervezői/kerületi összesítő (xlsx)](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Gyururendeles-2027-ra-Versenyszervezoi-Keruleti-osszesito-2.xlsx)
- Race example: Welsleben, 5 Jul 2025, "Dél-Kelet Magyarország (04-05-15-19-30) közös versenyei" with organisations 15 Békés (43 fanciers), 19 Törökszentmiklós (70), 30 Aranyhomok (77); ~190 fanciers, ~1,560 pigeons; club codes like N08, R161, P06, A41; each fancier shown with a 4-digit number (e.g. "Bedőcs Ferenc 7204") — [MyPigeons Hungary race result](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/rs/results/view/by-fancier/r-11271-t-122530-welsleben-regios/)
- Organiser numbers: "Hajdú" pigeons carry 10, "Szabolcs" 27 (in the ring) — [Agro Jager News, 8 Aug 2020](https://agrojager.hu/mezogazdasag/2020/08/08/elveszett-postagalambokat-keresnek/)
- Membership: "3987 tag" reported in the 140th-anniversary article (anniversary of 1882 → 2022) — [hiros.hu via search snippet](http://archiv.hiros.hu/hirek/140-eves-a-magyar-postagalamb-sportszovetseg---videoval) (page returned 403; figure seen only in search snippet). Older, undated federation pages give "több mint 5500 tag" / "közel 5500" — [archiv.postagalamb.hu (search snippet)](http://archiv.postagalamb.hu/?postagalambaszat); [postagalamb.wlap.hu](http://www.postagalamb.wlap.hu/)
- Decline confirmed by the federation's magazine (Sept 2026), Olympiad medallist Horváth István: "Egyre csökken a tagságunk" (our membership keeps falling) and this lowers Hungarian Olympiad coefficients — [Postagalambsport, 2026. szeptember](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PGS-szeptember_honlapra.pdf)
- Decline also described in farm press (2024): fewer fanciers as people turn to technology; youth recruitment difficulties — [Magyar Mezőgazdaság, 25 May 2024](https://magyarmezogazdasag.hu/2024/05/25/postagalamb-kulon-mufaj/)
- 2026 officials (magazine masthead): secretary-general Papdi Imre (Bordány); race committee acting chair Fejős Zsolt; health & international committee chair dr. Berta Krisztián — [PGS Sept 2026](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PGS-szeptember_honlapra.pdf)
- Extended board meeting 7 Nov 2026 had 8 of 11 members present; homepage carries open member letters disputing the 2026 Pannon Maraton results — [postagalambsport.hu](https://postagalambsport.hu/)
- Minors can be members with a parental declaration form ("Szülői nyilatkozat – kiskorú tagsági viszonyához") — [Nyomtatványok](https://postagalambsport.hu/nyomtatvanyok/)

### Inferences
- Addressable market is roughly 3,500–4,000 registered fanciers (2022 figure, likely lower in 2026), organised in a few dozen numbered organisers and a few hundred clubs. Each fancier, club and organiser already has a stable federation ID (fancier number, club code like "E-01", organiser number like "23"), which an app should store as fields.
- The data hierarchy an app should model: Federation → organiser (2-digit number + name) → club (letter-number code) → fancier (registration number) → loft (coordinates, max 2 fanciers per loft).

- No authoritative current (2025/2026) membership count or race-licence count found; the 140th-anniversary figure is from a snippet only. Annual counts are probably in the federation yearbook (Évkönyv) or general-assembly minutes, not read here.
- No complete list of organisers/districts with numbers; the old "A szervezet" page (archiv.postagalamb.hu) was unreachable (DNS failure).
- No age-profile data found anywhere.

## 2. Ring (gyűrű) system: format, ordering, register, ownership transfer, chip rings

### Takeaway
Hungarian rings read **HU-YYYY-OO-NNNNN**: country code, hatch year, two-digit organiser number, serial (e.g. HU-2023-29-14530, HU-2019-10-12345). Special series use a letter instead of the organiser number (seen: D, P, N, M, R), for Derby, Maraton/Pannon Maraton and national-colour rings. Rings are ordered once a year through club → organiser → federation, which orders from the manufacturer in July for the next year. There is no public ring register or transfer procedure; ownership is proven by the ring's certificate ("igazolólap"/"gyűrűkártya") or a written waiver. Chip rings must be writable Hitag S.

### Cited Findings
- Format explained: ring shows "az országkód … a kelési év, a szervezet kétjegyű száma" plus a multi-digit identifier; example "Hu-2019-10-12345"; Hajdú = 10, Szabolcs = 27 — [Agro Jager News, 2020](https://agrojager.hu/mezogazdasag/2020/08/08/elveszett-postagalambokat-keresnek/)
- Real 2026 example: Olympiad medallist "HU-2023-29-14530" (also written "HU2023-29-14530") — [PGS Sept 2026](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PGS-szeptember_honlapra.pdf)
- Real examples on the results portal: HU-2022-30-57280, HU-2023-19-02017, HU-2024-30-65453, HU-2023-15-56031 (numeric middle = organiser), and HU-2023-D-774566, HU-2023-P-44967 (letter middle); the middle segment does not equal the fancier's club code — [MyPigeons race result](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/rs/results/view/by-fancier/r-11271-t-122530-welsleben-regios/); further variants "HU-2021-D-629891" and letters N, M reported on Hungarian results — [search summary of MyPigeons results](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/rs/results/view/by-fancier/r-11271-t-122530-welsleben-regios/)
- Ring types and prices on the official 2027 ring order form: "versenyszervezői lábgyűrű" (organiser leg ring) 160 Ft/pc; "Derby gyűrű" 200 Ft/pc; "Nemzeti színű gyűrű" (national-colour ring) need not be pre-ordered; Maraton and Pannon Maraton rings are ordered by clubs directly from the federation — [Gyűrűrendelő lap 2027](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2027-gyururendeles.pdf)
- Ordering chain and calendar: the club ("A(z) …. jelű … Egyesület / Civil társaság", signed by club president) sends the form and payment to its organiser; quantities in multiples of 100; deadline to federation 1 July 2026, payment 31 August 2026; "a 2027. évi gyűrűket 2026 júliusában megrendeli a Sportszövetség a gyártónál" — [Gyűrűrendelő lap 2027](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2027-gyururendeles.pdf); organisers send a per-club summary (club code, headcount, organiser rings, Derby rings, amount) — [összesítő xlsx](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Gyururendeles-2027-ra-Versenyszervezoi-Keruleti-osszesito-2.xlsx)
- Race (rubber) rings: only federation-sold rubber race rings allowed; "A versenygyűrű forgalmazási rendje megegyezik a lábgyűrűk forgalmazásával"; federation sells only to tagszövetségek / versenykerületek / versenyszervezők; rubber rings carry 4+3 digits — [OSVSZ 2023, §6 3/](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Which leg rings may race: "magyar vagy külföldi leolvasható szabványos lábgyűrűvel", owner must prove ownership at base entry; non-FCI rings ordered in 2015 for 2016 are accepted; pigeons with rings older than 10 years may not race; a pigeon can be raced by only one fancier per season — [OSVSZ 2023, §5 4/, 6/](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Ownership proof: "A vitatott galamb tulajdoni viszonyának igazolására mindenkor a gyűrűhöz tartozó igazolólap, vagy a tulajdonos lemondó nyilatkozata szükséges"; an advert in the magazine or on galambom.hu gives racing rights, not ownership; at loft inspections the fancier must hold "a galamb gyűrűkártyájával, vagy a tulajdonjogot igazoló dokumentummal" — [OSVSZ 2023, §5 4/ and §8 3.1](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Foreign rings ("honosítás"): pigeons with foreign FCI-logo rings of year 2020 or later must be naturalised before racing: owner sends the ring certificate (igazolólap) with a request to the federation office; the secretary-general registers it; one-off fee 300 Ft/pigeon; rings 2019 or older exempt; exemption also for fanciers living beyond the borders who race in MPSZ member clubs. Unnaturalised birds → whole season's results deleted — [OSVSZ 2023, §5 4/](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Base entry ("alapnevezés"): each pigeon gets a code number that cannot change during the season; deadlines 15 April (old) / 31 August (young) on galambom.hu; only pigeons in the vaccination log may be entered; forms exist for manual (Word/Excel) and machine import (xlsx) — [OSVSZ 2023, §5 8/, §6 2/](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf); [Nyomtatványok](https://postagalambsport.hu/nyomtatvanyok/)
- Chip rings: electronic systems must be federation-approved and follow FCI electronic-clock rules; "csak a Hitag S jelű chipgyűrű használható"; chip ring must be writable; chip is linked to the permanent leg ring in the clock's base entry; chip replacement only before the club race committee, with new base entry sent to the organiser; pigeon codes must not change on chip swap — [OSVSZ 2023, §6 1/](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Lost/strayed pigeons: owner lookup via the Benzing "lost pigeons" page; full ring number required; only birds lost from official races are traceable — [Agro Jager News, 2020](https://agrojager.hu/mezogazdasag/2020/08/08/elveszett-postagalambokat-keresnek/); [MyPigeons Hungary lost](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/en/lost/)
- Separate body: MGKSz (Magyar Galamb- és Kisállattenyésztők Országos Szövetsége, fancy/ornamental pigeons) uses different rings marked "EE H" + size + year — not racing rings — [MGKSz gyűrűszámok](https://www.mgksz.hu/gyuruszamok-es-gyururendeles.html)

### Inferences
- An app's ring parser for Hungary should accept `HU-YYYY-NN-NNNNN` (2-digit organiser) and `HU-YYYY-L-NNNNNN` (letter series, serials up to 6 digits), with and without dashes/spaces. Letter meaning is likely D = Derby, P = Pannon Maraton, M = Maraton, N = Nemzeti színű (national ring) — inferred from the ring order form's ring types, not stated anywhere. R was also seen; meaning unknown.
- The organiser number in the ring tells the region of the breeder at ordering time, not the current owner's club; an app must store ring and current club separately.
- There is no digital ownership-transfer workflow at federation level; transfer = handing over the paper certificate (igazolólap / gyűrűkártya) or a signed waiver. An app could digitise this but cannot replace the paper proof under current rules.

### Gaps
- No official description of the igazolólap/gyűrűkártya fields (printed card from the ring maker? issued by club?) found; no form for it on the forms page.
- Ring manufacturer not named. Number of rings issued per year not found (would give a good proxy for active breeders).
- Official meaning of letter series (D, P, N, M, R) not documented in sources read.

## 3. Official documents: ownership card, pedigree format, who may issue pedigrees

### Takeaway
The only official per-pigeon document in the rules is the ring certificate ("gyűrűhöz tartozó igazolólap", called "gyűrűkártya" elsewhere in the same rules). MPSZ regulations do not define a pedigree (származási lap) format or restrict who may issue pedigrees; pedigrees are private documents made by breeders/sellers.

### Cited Findings
- Ownership proof = ring certificate ("igazolólap") or owner's waiver; loft inspectors check "gyűrűkártya" or other ownership document — [OSVSZ 2023, §5 4/ and §8 3.1](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Foreign birds' ownership is evidenced by the foreign "igazoló lap" stamped by the secretary-general during naturalisation — [OSVSZ 2023, §5 4/](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- The federation's list of regulations (statutes 2020, SZMSZ 2021, OSVSZ 2023, disciplinary 2021, judging & exhibition 2021, registration & transfer 2020, data protection 2021, dope rules) contains no pedigree regulation — [Szabályzatok](https://postagalambsport.hu/szabalyzatok/)
- Full-text search of OSVSZ 2023 for "szárm" (származás/származási) returned no pedigree rules (own search of the downloaded PDF text) — [OSVSZ 2023](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Federation issues certificates/diplomas ("Szövetségben kapható oklevelek") — [postagalambsport.hu, 28 Oct 2019](https://postagalambsport.hu/2019/10/28/szovetsegben-kaphato-oklevelek/) (not opened; title only from site menu)
- Magazine interviews present pedigree information informally (bloodline history, ring numbers, results) e.g. Horváth István's "30-as" line going back over three decades — [PGS Sept 2026](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PGS-szeptember_honlapra.pdf)

### Inferences
- A foreign pedigree app faces no regulatory gate on issuing/printing pedigrees in Hungary. Typical Hungarian pedigree content (inferred from magazine practice and international norms): ring number HU-…, name/nickname, sex, colour, breeder and loft, 2–4 generations of parents with ring numbers and race results (with organiser/club, FCI category, Olympiad/NB placings).
- Hungarian terms the UI needs: származási lap / pedigré, gyűrűszám, tenyésztő (breeder), dúc (loft), hím/tojó (cock/hen), szín (colour), apa/anya (sire/dam), eredmények.

### Gaps
- No source describing a standard Hungarian pedigree layout; no sample pedigree found. No information on whether the federation's yearbook/national exhibition requires a pedigree.

## 4. Results and data: portal, scoring software, clocks, championships, Olympiad, notable lofts

### Takeaway
All official race data must be processed and stored only in the federation's system **galambom.hu** (launched autumn 2015), which today is presented via the Benzing platform **mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary** (results 2016–2026, base entries, lost pigeons). Clocks with official readout guides: Benzing, Bricon, Mega, Tauris, Tipes (Topigeon named). National Championship (NB) scored from organiser races in short/middle/long categories; Hungary competes at the FCI Olympiad.

### Cited Findings
- "A hivatalos versenyek adatainak, eredményeinek feldolgozásához, az adatok tárolásához kizárólag a Sportszövetség által biztosított rendszer és felület – galambom.hu – használható"; organisers must upload results to galambom.hu within 8 days; race programme uploaded by 31 March; start videos (≥10 min) published on the galambom.hu results program within 7 days and kept public for 2 years — [OSVSZ 2023, §6 7/–8/, §7](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- galambom.hu launched autumn 2015, replacing a system where each organiser relied on one result calculator (a unified system had been used from 2006); public lists, results, base entries, statistics, maps, member stock data, "Galambjaim" (my pigeons) for registered users, lost-pigeon email matching; most electronic clocks supported, traditional clocks via Excel import — [postagalambsport.hu, 25 Apr 2016](https://postagalambsport.hu/2016/04/25/eredmenyszamolas-megujulva-galambom-hu/)
- Federation homepage banner links its results under a galambom.hu logo to Benzing's "mypigeons" Hungary portal and the lost-pigeon finder — [postagalambsport.hu](https://postagalambsport.hu/); results pages exist for 2016–2026 for "O-50 Magyar Postagalamb Sportszövetség" — [MyPigeons 2026](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/hu/results/2026/o-50-magyar-postagalamb-sportsz-vetseg/), [MyPigeons 2016](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/hu/results/2016/o-50-magyar-postagalamb-sportszovetseg/); contact info@galambom.hu — [MyPigeons contact](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/en/contact/); portal uses Benzing OAuth login, ~25 languages, admin panel, liberation sites, lost pigeons — [MyPigeons 2026](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/hu/results/2026/o-50-magyar-postagalamb-sportsz-vetseg/); Benzing publishes a data-import manual — [MyPigeons data import guide](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/en/guide/data-import/)
- Base-entry (alapnevezés) lists are hosted there too (2023) and login-protected — [MyPigeons alapnevezés 2023](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/hu/pigeon-listing/2023/o-50-magyar-postagalamb-sportsz-vetseg/) (redirects to Benzing OAuth login)
- Result tables: per fancier (name + number, club code, distance, basketed/clocked, points) and per pigeon (entry no., ring, arrival, speed, coefficient); 20% prize rate (FCI rule) — [MyPigeons Welsleben 2025](https://mypigeons.benzing.live/hungary/rs/results/view/by-fancier/r-11271-t-122530-welsleben-regios/); [OSVSZ 2023 §… 6/ "díjlistára kerül az indított galambok 20 százaléka (FCI szabály)"](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Clock readout guides on the federation site: Benzing, Bricon (all types), Mega, Tauris, Tipes; Topigeon named without guide; Unikon not mentioned — [Versenyadatok kiolvasása elektronikus órákból](https://postagalambsport.hu/versenykiirasok/versenyadatok-kiolvasasa-elektronikus-orakbol/)
- Rules allow traditional clocks with rubber rings or federation-approved chip systems (club antenna, master clock or GPS receiver, loft antenna); systems must transmit race data electronically; FCI-approved LIVE systems recognised — [OSVSZ 2023, §6 1/ and later sections](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- National Championship (Nemzeti Bajnokság, NB): announced by 30 January each year; computed from organiser races accepted in April; 2026 programme template "NB 8-8-4": 8 short (R), 8 middle (K), 4 long (H) races plus Maraton — [OSVSZ 2023, §4 12/, §5 8/](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf); [Versenyprogram kiírás 2026 (xlsx)](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Versenyprogram-2026-NB-8-8-4-.xlsx). Other federation events: Magyar Kupa, Nemzeti Kiállítás, Évkönyv (yearbook) — [Átigazolási szabályzat 2020, pt. 7](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/MPS-Vegleges-atigazolasi-szabalyzat-uj-2020-10-21.pdf); national races 2026 from Liberec (yearlings) and Rostock — [Nyomtatványok](https://postagalambsport.hu/nyomtatvanyok/)
- FCI evaluation groups ("FCI 1, FCI 2…", min. 20 fanciers) and race groups (min. 40); FCI minimum per race 20 fanciers / 200 pigeons — [OSVSZ 2023](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Olympiad: Horváth István's HU-2023-29-14530 ("a 30-as") took 3rd place in Sport I category (races over 500 km) at the most recent FCI Olympiad, reported Sept 2026; he joined the federation in 1980 — [PGS Sept 2026](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PGS-szeptember_honlapra.pdf)
- Marathon clubs exist (e.g. Postagalamb Maraton Klub, international Paris race co-organised by the Romanian federation) — [postagalambmaratonklub.hu](https://postagalambmaratonklub.hu/hirek/55-parizs-nemzetkozi-verseny); Pannon Maraton young-bird race 3 Oct 2026 — [postagalambsport.hu](https://postagalambsport.hu/)

### Inferences
- Benzing is the incumbent data partner: it hosts the federation's mandatory results/registry platform. Pedigree data (results per ring) live in MyPigeons; an app that imports results by ring number from MyPigeons/galambom.hu (or accepts the federation's base-entry Excel format) would match how Hungarian fanciers already work.
- The exact relationship galambom.hu ↔ Benzing (vendor-built? migrated?) is not stated; treat as "galambom.hu brand, Benzing MyPigeons platform".

### Gaps
- No public list of top Hungarian lofts or NB champions was retrieved (yearbook/NB pages not opened). A 2024 article about a Hungarian pigeon world record exists ([Magyar Mezőgazdaság, 13 Feb 2024](https://magyarmezogazdasag.hu/2024/02/13/postagalamb-magyar-vilagrekord/)) but was not read.
- Market share of clock brands in Hungary not found.

## 5. MPSZ rules on software, data, GDPR, vendor partnerships

### Takeaway
MPSZ has a 2021 data-protection policy plus five data-processing notices, requires each racing member's written GDPR consent, and mandates galambom.hu as the exclusive system for official race data. Clock/chip systems need federation approval. No rule found about third-party pedigree apps.

### Cited Findings
- Documents: Adatvédelmi Szabályzat (2021.07.06) and data notices on awards, contact data, race licence/registration/transfer, board members, member register (all 2021.07.06) — [Szabályzatok](https://postagalambsport.hu/szabalyzatok/)
- Race participation requires that the member "a Sportszövetség adatkezelési szabályzatát megismerte, elfogadta és személyes adatainak kezeléséhez hozzájáruló, írásbeli nyilatkozatot tett" — [OSVSZ 2023, §5 1/](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Exclusive use of galambom.hu for official race data processing and storage; results public (start videos public for 2 years) — [OSVSZ 2023, §6 7/](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Electronic systems usable only per FCI rules and federation approval; race committees withdraw unfit clocks and inform members via the magazine — [OSVSZ 2023, §6 1/](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Federation sells advertising (magazine "Postagalambsport", monthly; 2024 rate card) — [Hirdetési tájékoztató 2024](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/hirdetesi_tajekoztato_2024.pdf) (title only); magazine archive 2024–2026 — [Szaklap archívum](https://postagalambsport.hu/ujsagarchivum/)

### Inferences
- A pedigree/loft app is outside the federation's regulated scope (it does not calculate official results), so no approval is needed; but official results must come from galambom.hu/MyPigeons. A partnership route would be the federation (magazine ads, possibly data access) or Benzing.
- Public results show fancier names; an app reusing them in the EU should still treat them as personal data under GDPR.

### Gaps
- The data-protection policy PDFs were not read in full; unknown whether they say anything about data sharing with third parties or APIs.

## 6. Hungarian-speaking fanciers in Romania (Transylvania)

### Takeaway
Transylvanian Hungarian fanciers have their own clubs registered in Romania (e.g. Udvarhelyszéki Postagalambászok Egyesülete / Asociația Columbofililor din Zona Odorheiu Secuiesc, 2006) and in general race in the Romanian federations with RO rings; joint Romanian–Hungarian races exist. MPSZ rules explicitly allow fanciers living beyond the borders to race inside MPSZ clubs with foreign rings. No ethnic breakdown numbers found.

### Cited Findings
- Udvarhelyszéki Postagalambászok Egyesülete, Székelyudvarhely, founded 2006, Romanian name Asociația Columbofililor Din Zona Odorheiu Secuiesc — [ErdélyStat intézménytár](https://intezmenytar.erdelystat.ro/intezmenyek/romaniai-magyar-civil-szervezetek/udvarhelyszeki-postagalambaszok-egyesulete/20929)
- Romanian federation ("Román Postagalambsport-szövetség") held its 37th national exhibition in Brașov, has "több mint négyezer tag", president Florin Moldovan (article date not verified) — [Krónika](https://kronika.ro/szines/postagalamb_kavalkad_a_cenk_alatt)
- Romanian and Hungarian breeders organise many joint races; e.g. 3,147 Romanian and Hungarian pigeons released at Würzburg — [Krónika, Maratoni röptetés](https://kronika.ro/szines/maratoni_roptetes)
- Romania total: about 8,000 registered fanciers in the two large federations; the FCI-affiliated body is the Uniunea Federațiilor Columbofile din România (date unclear) — [Jurnalul](https://jurnalul.ro/sport/columbofilie-pasi-mari-pe-taram-nou-91193.html); "Columba" federation, vice-president Florin Gătejescu (2022) — [puterea.ro](https://www.puterea.ro/?p=177243)
- MPSZ exemption: foreign-ringed pigeons of "a határainkon túl élő, a Magyar Postagalamb Sportszövetség jogi személyiségű tagjainak soraiban versenyző természetes személy tagok" need no naturalisation — [OSVSZ 2023, §5 4/](http://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/OSVSZ-2023.01.27.-TELJES-ELFOGADOTT.pdf)
- Nagyvárad (Oradea) fancier meetings with bird exchanges reported by Hungarian-language Romanian press — [ERDON](https://www.erdon.ro/cimke/roman-postagalamb-szovetseg)

### Inferences
- A Romanian-made app entering Hungary can use Transylvanian Hungarian clubs as a bridge market: same language, RO rings at home, and some race in MPSZ clubs (rules provide for it). The app must handle mixed RO/HU rings in one loft.
- Romanian federation naming in sources is inconsistent (Uniunea Federațiilor Columbofile, "Columba", a second "professional" federation); "AC Romania"/"FC Columbofilie" names asked about were not confirmed by any source found.

### Gaps
- No count of Hungarian-speaking fanciers in Romania, nor of Transylvanian members racing in MPSZ clubs.
- Not verified which Romanian federation(s) the Transylvanian Hungarian clubs belong to, or their ring prefixes.
