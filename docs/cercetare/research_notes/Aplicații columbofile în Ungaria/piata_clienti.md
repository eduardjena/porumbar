# Hungarian pigeon fanciers as customers: people, channels, willingness to pay, opportunities and risks for a phone pedigree app

Research date: 2026-11-18. About 17 searches and fetches, in Hungarian and English. Coverage is uneven: the federations and auction sites are well indexed, but Facebook groups, membership fees and software prices are not. Older data is marked with its year.

## 1. Who the fanciers are: age, rural vs urban, phone and internet use

### Takeaway
Hungary has roughly 5,500 racing-pigeon fanciers (MPSZ figure, probably from around 2009-2010 and likely lower now) plus about 2,000 fancy/breed-pigeon breeders in MGKSZ. The scene is a small-town and village hobby organised in more than 300 local clubs, and numbers are declining. Older Hungarians are now mostly online up to about 75, but only about 57% of 65+ use the internet on a phone, and many prefer a desktop PC because phone text is too small. That supports a "big text, phone-first" design but also means a PC/print path matters.

### Cited Findings
- MPSZ (Magyar Postagalamb Sportszövetség, racing pigeons) was founded in 1882 and is "one of Europe's oldest". It has 5,500 members, 29 county-level member federations (tagszövetség) and more than 300 local clubs. Undated municipal page; its internal references ("128 years") point to about 2009-2010. — [Szob város, Galambászat](https://szob.hu/galambaszat/)
- MGKSZ (Magyar Galamb- és Kisállattenyésztők Országos Szövetsége, fancy/breed pigeons and small animals) says its pigeon breeders number "about 2,000". It also says pigeon fancier numbers are falling across Europe, Hungary included, while poultry breeders are increasing. The page is undated. — [MGKSZ](https://www.mgksz.hu/)
- An example local club (Nagydorog, 2024) has 22 members (18 pigeon fanciers): 6 retirees, 3 schoolchildren and 13 working members. Membership has ranged from under 10 to over 30 since about 1978. — [Magyar Mezőgazdaság, 2024-04-17](https://magyarmezogazdasag.hu/2024/04/17/galambok-negyvenot-ev-nagydorogi-egyesuletben/)
- In the Szob club (founded 1979, 12 founding members), membership fluctuated over 30 years because of rising keeping and competition costs, regulations and family circumstances. The club meets in the fire-station depot and races run April-September. — [Szob város](https://szob.hu/galambaszat/)
- Internet use by age (NMHH, reported 2024): close to 100% for 15-45. Ages 60-65 were above 80% in 2023, up 25 percentage points since 2019, with strong growth continuing up to 75. Under 10% of those 80+ use the internet, unchanged in four years. — [Economx, 2024-05-28, citing NMHH](https://www.economx.hu/belfold/2024/05/28/idosek-internethasznalat-vegzettseg-nmhh-munkavegzes-790430/)
- Mobile internet: 57% of those 65+ use the internet on a mobile phone, against 87% of teenagers. Older people more often go online with a desktop PC, and the article attributes this to small phone text and discomfort with touchscreens. — [Economx/NMHH 2024](https://www.economx.hu/belfold/2024/05/28/idosek-internethasznalat-vegzettseg-nmhh-munkavegzes-790430/)
- Education gap: among 70-year-olds, 48% with only primary education use the internet, against 65% of graduates. People still working past retirement age are about 15 points more likely to be online. An internet-using household member strongly raises adoption. — [Economx/NMHH 2024](https://www.economx.hu/belfold/2024/05/28/idosek-internethasznalat-vegzettseg-nmhh-munkavegzes-790430/)
- Among Hungarian 16+ internet users, 91% used a smartphone in 2024 (NMHH; no 65+ breakdown found in the snippet). — [NMHH internethasználói kutatás 2024](https://nmhh.hu/dokumentum/251289/internethasznaloi_kutatas_2024_akadalymentes.pdf)
- Online shopping: about 27% of Hungarians aged 65-74 bought online in 2025, against an EU average of about 35% for that age group. This comes from a secondary table citing Eurostat. — [search summary of Eurostat-based tables; see Eurostat "Digitalisation in Europe 2026"](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/fr/web/interactive-publications/digitalisation-2026)
- KSH 2020 (old): in the 65-74 band men were online slightly more than women (55% vs 52%). — [KSH Digitális társadalom 2020](https://www.ksh.hu/docs/hun/xftp/idoszaki/ikt/2020/01/digitalis_tarsadalom_2020.pdf)

### Inferences
- The realistic addressable market in Hungary is small: about 5,500 racing fanciers (likely fewer by 2026) plus about 2,000 breed-pigeon breeders. Even 5% paid adoption would mean only a few hundred subscribers.
- Clubs have a mixed age profile with a heavy retiree share. The decision-maker is often a 60-75-year-old man in a village or small town, and frequently a younger relative or club member is the one who "does the computer". That fits Porumbar's elderly focus, and suggests selling through the helper (son, grandson, club secretary).
- Only 57% of 65+ use mobile internet, and many prefer a PC because text is small. Big text is a real differentiator, but the app must also work well on a desktop browser, which a web app already does.

### Gaps
- No current (2025-2026) MPSZ membership number was found. The federation's general assembly papers or the monthly "Postagalambsport" magazine would have it.
- No age distribution of Hungarian fanciers was found.
- No KSH/Eurostat 2024-2025 figure for smartphone ownership among 65+ in Hungary was found directly. Check Eurostat isoc_ci_dev_i or the KSH STADAT IKT tables.
- No urban/rural split for fanciers was found. The rural/small-town character is inferred from club pages.

## 2. Where they gather online and offline

### Takeaway
The structure is formal and federation-centred. MPSZ runs a 29-region, 300+ club network, the monthly magazine "Postagalambsport" comes with membership, and the National Show is held every winter in Lakitelek. Online activity centres on the Hungarian auction sites (galambaukcio.hu, galamblicit.hu), the results site galambom.hu, classifieds (Jófogás, Adokveszek), small club websites (gportal, 5mp.eu) and Facebook. The Facebook groups found are small (hundreds of members).

### Cited Findings
- **National show:** the MPSZ 68th Országos Postagalamb Kiállítás and 9th Central Europe Cup (Közép-Európa Kupa) ran 30 Jan-2 Feb 2025 at Hungarikum Liget, Lakitelek. Entry was 6,000 Ft and a full catalogue was published online. — [postagalambsport.hu, kiírás PDF](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/68.-ORSZAGOS-KIALLITAS-es-9.-Kozep-Europa-kupa-kiiras-.pdf); [postagalambsport.hu photos](https://postagalambsport.hu/2025/02/07/68-orszagos-kiallitas-9-kozep-europa-kupa-kepek/); [Hungarikum Liget](https://hungarikumliget.hu/cikkek/rendezveny/68-orszagos-postagalamb-kiallitas-video)
- **Breed-pigeon show:** the MGKSZ National and National Young Pigeon, Poultry, Rabbit and Ornamental Bird Show ran 21-23 Feb 2025 in Cegléd (Living Sport hall). — [MGKSZ nemzeti kiállítás](https://www.mgksz.hu/nemzeti-kiallitas-.html)
- **Regional shows:** district (kerületi) shows are held every year under the MPSZ national-show rules, for example in Kecskemét (30. Aranyhomok club). — [Aranyhomok klub](https://30aranyhomokklub.5mp.eu/web.php?a=30aranyhomokklub&o=iNcAUo8dbk)
- **Magazine:** MPSZ publishes "Postagalambsport" monthly as PDF issues on postagalambsport.hu (for example a January 2026 issue). According to an MGKSZ forum thread, the magazine comes with membership. — [PGS január 2026 PDF](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/PGS-januar_honlapra.pdf); [MGKSZ forum](https://mgksz.hu/index.php?no=27&oldal=hozzaszolasok&page=forum&topic=134)
- **Regional federation sites** exist, for example the 15th Békés Tagszövetség, Hajdú Tagszövetség and Kolumbia Tagszövetség. — [bekestagszovetseg.hu](https://bekestagszovetseg.hu/); [kolumbiatagszovetseg.hu](https://kolumbiatagszovetseg.hu/egyesuletek)
- **Facebook:** the official MPSZ page had about 2,731 followers. Example trading groups: "Hungary-Verseny Postagalamb tenyésztés" (533 members) and "Postagalambászat kellékei adás-vétel" (728 members). The counts come from a mirror page and may be stale. — [MPSZ Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/p/Magyar-Postagalamb-Sportsz%C3%B6vets%C3%A9g-100063121215026/); [5mp.eu mirror](http://www.5mp.eu/fajlok2/szabozoo/galamb_adas_es_vetel_www.5mp.eu_.html)
- Group moderators require posts to state the price and town, and delete posts selling trapped (caught) birds. — [5mp.eu mirror](http://www.5mp.eu/fajlok2/szabozoo/galamb_adas_es_vetel_www.5mp.eu_.html)
- **Classifieds:** many current private sale listings are on Jófogás and Adokveszek. — [Jófogás postagalamb](https://www.jofogas.hu/magyarorszag?q=postagalamb); [Adokveszek](https://www.adokveszek.hu/kereses?mit=h%C3%A1zi+galamb)
- **Online auctions:**
  - galambaukcio.hu auctions only birds that have a photo and a pedigree (származási lap). It is available in six languages, and it has scheduled sales such as "Derby Hungary Auction 3" and "November Mix" (22 Nov 2026, 39 active pigeons) and is in its "VI. Szezon". — [galambaukcio.hu Rólunk](https://galambaukcio.hu/rolunk); [galambaukcio.hu](https://galambaukcio.hu/)
  - galamblicit.hu runs racing-pigeon auctions with bidding or a fixed "villámár" (buy-now) price. — [galamblicit.hu](https://galamblicit.hu/)
  - pigeon.plus has an auction module backed by FCI Grand Prix Vác. — [pigeonped.com/hu](https://pigeonped.com/hu/)
- **Results database:** galambom.hu is described as the place where "all results are now available". Buyers use it to check breeders before bidding. — [galambaukcio.hu article](https://galambaukcio.hu/a-galamb-aukcios-oldal-szerepe-magyar-postagalambsportban/)
- **Buying season:** autumn, when breeders sell young birds after the season. Buyers research 2-3 trusted breeders' results first. — [galambaukcio.hu article](https://galambaukcio.hu/a-galamb-aukcios-oldal-szerepe-magyar-postagalambsportban/)
- Many clubs and individual fanciers run old-style free websites (gportal.hu, 5mp.eu), including "adok-veszek" (buy-sell) pages. — [okroskalman.gportal.hu](https://okroskalman.gportal.hu/gindex.php?pg=23312004); [3aszpostagalambasz.gportal.hu](https://3aszpostagalambasz.gportal.hu/gindex.php?pg=8294310)

### Inferences
- The highest-leverage channels are the national show at Lakitelek (late Jan/early Feb: a stand or flyers, with the A4 pedigree with QR shown physically), ads or an article in the monthly "Postagalambsport" magazine, the 29 tagszövetségek and the auction sites. A partnership in which a QR pedigree is accepted as the "származási lap" for auction listings would be strong.
- The autumn buying season (Oct-Nov) is when people make pedigrees for sales, which makes it the natural launch or promotion window.
- Facebook groups are fragmented and small, so they are useful for word of mouth but not as a mass channel.

### Gaps
- A comprehensive list of Hungarian pigeon Facebook groups with current member counts was not found, because Facebook groups are poorly indexed. A manual check on Facebook is needed.
- No major Hungarian YouTube channels were identified (not searched in depth).
- No PIPA-specific data on the Hungarian share of buyers or sellers was found.
- Magazine circulation and ad prices were not found.

## 3. How they handle pedigrees today

### Takeaway
Pedigrees (származási lap / törzslap) are central to Hungarian sales: galambaukcio.hu requires one for every bird. A Hungarian web tool (pigeon.plus, formerly "pped") already makes printable pedigrees with family trees. Hand-written and Windows-based practices were not documented by sources found here.

### Cited Findings
- galambaukcio.hu auctions only birds with a photo and a pedigree, and shows each bird's pedigree with the ancestors' race results. Buyers favour pedigrees with many top performers on both the sire and dam lines. — [galambaukcio.hu Rólunk](https://galambaukcio.hu/rolunk); [galambaukcio.hu article](https://galambaukcio.hu/a-galamb-aukcios-oldal-szerepe-magyar-postagalambsportban/)
- **pigeon.plus** (pigeonped.com/hu, formerly "pped") is Hungarian-made and Hungarian-first, with an English (UK) version. It offers:
  - bird records with photos and a family tree
  - "AutoFill" of ancestors by searching other breeders' records
  - pedigree sheets in several styles with the loft logo, printed directly or exported to image/PDF, with an optional inbreeding highlight
  - a loft manager covering nest boxes, eggs, hatching, ringing and foster parents
  - an auction module

  The site lists a sole-proprietor registration number (EV-56448665) and is "Made by Ceberit". The page shows no pricing, QR code or phone app. — [pigeonped.com/hu](https://pigeonped.com/hu/)
- Classified (Jófogás) listings mostly do not mention pedigrees. — [Jófogás](https://www.jofogas.hu/magyarorszag?q=postagalamb) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- The A4 pedigree is already a cultural norm in Hungary, especially for auctions. A QR plus public link that lets a buyer verify the pedigree online fits well with auction practice.
- pigeon.plus is the direct local competitor on pedigrees and family trees. Porumbar's differentiators would be pedigree-from-photo (AI reading of an existing paper pedigree), very large text, phone-first use, and QR verification and share links.

### Gaps
- No sources confirmed the share of fanciers using hand-written pedigrees, Excel, Windows programs (for example Benzing / Pigeon Planner / international software) or Messenger/Viber photos. This is likely common but unverified.
- pigeon.plus pricing, user count and whether it has a free tier were not found.

## 4. Willingness to pay

### Takeaway
The hobby's fixed costs are low and paid in small HUF amounts: rings of 80-160 Ft each, a racing licence of 2,000 Ft, 50 Ft per entered pigeon and a 6,000 Ft show ticket. Fanciers do spend on birds at auctions. A ~30 EUR/year subscription (~12,000 Ft) is larger than many of these line items, so it needs clear value. VAT is 27% for B2C digital services in Hungary, the highest in the EU.

### Cited Findings
- MGKSZ 2025 rings: 80 Ft each (2.5-16 mm) for members and 160 Ft for non-members; 100 or 200 Ft for 18-27 mm. Late orders cost double. Members pay the federation fee through their club, which also gives them their breeder number (törzsszám). — [MGKSZ gyűrűrendelés 2025](https://www.mgksz.hu/gyururendeles---2025.html)
- Racing rings (2025, regional federation): 120 Ft each, or 160 Ft for Derby rings. — [Hajdú tagszövetség gyűrűrendelő](http://www.hajdutagszovetseg.hu/dl.php?id=6556)
- Regional racing fees (2025): racing licence (versenyengedély) 2,000 Ft, 50 Ft per entered pigeon, rubber ring 25 Ft. — [postagalamb26.hu 2025 alapnevezés](http://postagalamb26.hu/2025/02/26/2025-alapnevezes-leadasa-hataridok/)
- National show entry: 6,000 Ft (2025). — [kiírás PDF](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/68.-ORSZAGOS-KIALLITAS-es-9.-Kozep-Europa-kupa-kiiras-.pdf)
- Rising "keeping and competition costs" are cited as a reason members drop out, and a club applied for municipal grants to cover members' race costs. — [Szob város](https://szob.hu/galambaszat/)
- Hungary's standard VAT is 27%, which applies to SaaS and electronically supplied services sold B2C. The €10,000 EU-wide threshold for cross-border B2C e-services means that below it an EU seller may charge home-country VAT, and above it, VAT of the customer's country applies, declared through the EU OSS return. These are commercial tax-guide sources, not confirmed against NAV or the European Commission. — [Fonoa Hungary digital services](https://www.fonoa.com/resources/country-tax-guides/hungary/tax-on-digital-services); [Kintsugi EU VAT 2026](https://trykintsugi.com/sales-tax-guides/europe/european-union); [Commenda Hungary OSS](https://www.commenda.io/hungary/vat-one-stop-shop-oss)

### Inferences
- About 30 EUR/year is roughly 2-3 national-show tickets, or the ring cost for about 100 young birds. That is plausible for active breeders who sell birds, but likely too much for casual retirees. A cheaper Hungary price point, or a free tier with paid printing/QR, may be needed.
- Sellers on auctions (who need pedigrees) are the segment with the clearest ROI.
- On VAT: if Porumbar's Hungarian B2C sales plus all other EU cross-border B2C sales stay under €10,000/year, Romanian VAT rules apply. Above that, 27% Hungarian VAT applies through OSS, which would eat into a fixed EUR price. Unverified: OSS registration requires the seller to be VAT-registered in Romania, so a non-VAT-registered Romanian micro-enterprise would need to check this with an accountant.
- Invoicing: Hungarian consumers do not need a Hungarian "számla" from a foreign seller, but the Romanian invoice/receipt should be clear and bilingual. This is an inference and was not verified.

### Gaps
- MPSZ annual membership fee and club fee amounts were not found.
- No prices were found for existing Hungarian pigeon software, including pigeon.plus.
- No data on payment habits (card vs bank transfer vs cash) among older Hungarian fanciers. NMHH/MNB payment surveys could fill this.
- Auction price levels in Hungary were not captured.
- No official NAV or EC page was fetched for OSS. Verify with the European Commission's OSS portal and an accountant.

## 5. Language, trust and the Romanian origin

### Takeaway
No direct evidence was found. The Hungarian ecosystem (federation, auction sites, pigeon.plus) is Hungarian-first, which implies that a fully Hungarian UI and Hungarian-language support are a must for older users.

### Cited Findings
- pigeon.plus is primarily Hungarian, and its auction is "currently Hungarian only". — [pigeonped.com/hu](https://pigeonped.com/hu/)
- galambaukcio.hu runs in six languages, which shows that international buyers and sellers are part of the Hungarian scene. — [galambaukcio.hu article](https://galambaukcio.hu/a-galamb-aukcios-oldal-szerepe-magyar-postagalambsportban/)
- The Central Europe Cup (Közép-Európa Kupa) is held at the Hungarian national show, which suggests regional cross-border participation. — [kiírás PDF](https://postagalambsport.hu/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/68.-ORSZAGOS-KIALLITAS-es-9.-Kozep-Europa-kupa-kiiras-.pdf)

### Inferences
- Older fanciers with limited digital skills almost certainly need a fully Hungarian UI, with Hungarian terms such as származási lap, gyűrűszám, tenyésztő, dúc and hím/tojó, plus Hungarian support by phone or Viber/Messenger.
- A Romanian-made product may face some trust friction in Hungary, but a Hungarian-speaking face (for example a Transylvanian Hungarian representative) would likely neutralise it. Unverified.

### Gaps
- No sources were found on attitudes of Hungarian fanciers toward Romanian-made products. Interviews or Facebook group polling would be needed.

## 6. Hungarian-speaking fanciers in Romania (Transylvania)

### Takeaway
Hungarian-speaking pigeon clubs exist in Transylvania. One example is the Udvarhelyszéki Postagalambászok Egyesülete in Székelyudvarhely (Odorheiu Secuiesc), founded in 2006. They would use Romanian (FCI RO) rings, Romanian pricing and payment, and a Hungarian UI. This makes them a logical bridge market, but no size data was found.

### Cited Findings
- The Udvarhelyszéki Postagalambászok Egyesülete (Asociația Columbofililor din Zona Odorheiu Secuiesc), in Hargita county, was founded in 2006 and is listed in the Erdélystat Hungarian civil-organisation directory. — [Erdélystat intézménytár](https://intezmenytar.erdelystat.ro/intezmenyek/romaniai-magyar-civil-szervezetek/udvarhelyszeki-postagalambaszok-egyesulete/20929)

### Inferences
- A Transylvanian Hungarian rollout needs only a Hungarian UI on the existing Romanian setup: RO rings, lei pricing, Romanian invoicing, no OSS. It tests the Hungarian translation and builds Hungarian-language testimonials before entering Hungary.
- The Erdélystat directory could be mined for more Hungarian pigeon associations in Harghita, Covasna, Mureș, Bihor, Satu Mare and Cluj.

### Gaps
- No count of Hungarian-speaking fanciers in Romania was found. Search Romanian federation (FC Columbofilă) branch lists and Erdélystat.
- No clubs were found for Kovászna (Covasna) or Szatmár (Satu Mare) in this pass.

## 7. Competitive gaps a simple app could fill

### Takeaway
The main local competitor, pigeon.plus, already covers pedigrees, family trees, print/PDF and loft management in Hungarian. Gaps that remain visible are pedigree-from-photo (AI digitisation of existing paper pedigrees), QR/link verification of printed pedigrees for auctions and sales, big-text phone-first use for seniors, and a simple sales list. No source confirmed that these are missing elsewhere.

### Cited Findings
- pigeon.plus features: photos, family tree, ancestor AutoFill from other breeders' data, styled pedigree print/PDF/image, inbreeding highlighting, loft manager, auctions. The page shows no QR, no phone app and no photo-to-pedigree import. — [pigeonped.com/hu](https://pigeonped.com/hu/)
- Auctions require a pedigree and a photo per bird, and buyers cross-check results on galambom.hu. — [galambaukcio.hu Rólunk](https://galambaukcio.hu/rolunk); [galambaukcio.hu article](https://galambaukcio.hu/a-galamb-aukcios-oldal-szerepe-magyar-postagalambsportban/)
- 65+ Hungarians find phone text too small and touchscreens uncomfortable. — [Economx/NMHH 2024](https://www.economx.hu/belfold/2024/05/28/idosek-internethasznalat-vegzettseg-nmhh-munkavegzes-790430/)

### Inferences
- Positioning options:
  - "Turn your paper pedigrees into digital ones by photo": addresses older fanciers' backlog of paper records.
  - "Verifiable A4 pedigree with QR for auctions and sales": possibly linked to the galambom.hu results.
  - "Readable for seniors": big text and few buttons.
- Risk: pigeon.plus's AutoFill network effect (shared ancestor database) is strong in a small market. Porumbar would compete against an incumbent with local data.
- Risk: the market is small and declining (about 5,500 or fewer racing fanciers, about 2,000 breed fanciers). Hungary may be worth only a few hundred paying users, so customer acquisition must be cheap (shows, magazine, federations).

### Gaps
- No list of other software used in Hungary (international Windows programs, Benzing/Tauris club systems, mobile apps) or their prices was verified.
- No user reviews or complaints about pigeon.plus were found.
