# Racing-pigeon ring number formats in East and Southeast Asia (CHN, TW, JPN, KOR, THA, PH, MY, ID, VN, HK)

Research date: 2026-11-20. "Verified" means the text was read directly on the cited page (most were downloaded with curl because WebFetch got 403/DNS errors). "Via search snippet" means only the search engine's excerpt was seen, not the page itself.

Most useful single source: the **Pattaya International Pigeon Race (PIPR) participant list** (Thailand, 2026 season, ~2,070 rings, all with year "26"). The page embeds JSON (`"country":"CN","pigeons":[{"ring_no":"CHN 26-15-0443185"}...]`) and gives one organiser's normalised writing for every Asian country that took part — [PIPR participants](https://www.pattayaoneloftrace.com/en/participant). Every PIPR ring is written `PREFIX YY-REST`, with a space after the prefix, a 2-digit year and hyphens. That is the one-loft and "international" writing; it is not necessarily what is printed on the ring.

---

## 1. China (mainland, CHN): official format, province codes, real examples

### Takeaway
The official ring is **CHN + 4-digit year + 2-digit provincial/sector association code + 7-digit serial**, plus a QR code or colour barcode. Rings from before 2014 had a **6-digit** serial. It is usually written `2019-23-0228879` or `CHN 2026-01-1126801`. One-loft and international lists write it `CHN 26-01-1126801`. No Chinese characters are part of the number: the codes are digits, and the Chinese text appears only on the ownership certificate (足环证).

### Cited Findings
- The revised CXA/中国信鸽协会 regulation 《中国信鸽足环管理规定（修订版）》, Art. 9, says ring information must include: "（一）中国的英文缩写CHN(3位英文字母)；（二）发行年的年份(4位阿拉伯数字)；（三）省级协会代码（2位阿拉伯数字）（四）序号（7位数字）（五）二维码或颜色条形信息码" — [chinaxinge baike 7607](http://news.chinaxinge.com/baike/view.asp?id=7607&sjm=y1e0tamt3l7k). Page created 2018-02-28. The regulation's own date was not visible on the page.
- The same regulation, Art. 8: ring with inner diameter 8 mm, outer 10.8 mm, height 11 mm, aluminium inner layer and transparent plastic outer layer. Colours rotate yearly among 8 base colours (red 185c, orange 804c, yellow 113c, apple green 375c, pink 806c, blue 638c, purple 528c, grass green 3395c), "自2019年开始执行" — [same source](http://news.chinaxinge.com/baike/view.asp?id=7607&sjm=y1e0tamt3l7k).
- Ownership certificate (足环证, Art. 10): it carries "CHINA", "CHN", the 4-digit year, the 2-digit association code, a "流水顺序编号（7位阿拉伯数字）" and a "校验码（6位阿拉伯数字）". The Chinese text 中国信鸽协会 / 信鸽足环证 / 校验码 is on the certificate, not in the number — [same source](http://news.chinaxinge.com/baike/view.asp?id=7607&sjm=y1e0tamt3l7k).
- **Association code table (附件1 全国统一足环协会代码表), verbatim:** 00 中国信鸽协会专属特比、纪念环 (CXA special-race and commemorative rings); 01 北京 Beijing; 02 天津 Tianjin; 03 河北 Hebei; 04 山西 Shanxi; 05 内蒙 Inner Mongolia; 06 辽宁 Liaoning; 07 吉林 Jilin; 08 黑龙江 Heilongjiang; 09 上海 Shanghai; 10 江苏 Jiangsu; 11 浙江 Zhejiang; 12 安徽 Anhui; 13 福建 Fujian; 14 江西 Jiangxi; 15 山东 Shandong; 16 河南 Henan; 17 湖北 Hubei; 18 湖南 Hunan; 19 广东 Guangdong; 20 广西 Guangxi; 21 海南 Hainan; 22 四川 Sichuan; 23 贵州 Guizhou; 24 云南 Yunnan; 25 西藏 Tibet; 26 陕西 Shaanxi; 27 甘肃 Gansu; 28 青海 Qinghai; 29 宁夏 Ningxia; 30 新疆 Xinjiang; 31 重庆 Chongqing; 32 火车头 ("Locomotive", the China railway-sector pigeon association); 33 石油 (petroleum-sector association) — [same source](http://news.chinaxinge.com/baike/view.asp?id=7607&sjm=y1e0tamt3l7k). Code 00 was added in the revision ("增加了中国信鸽协会专属特比、特定环，代码为00").
- Codes 32 and 33 are sector associations, not provinces: "代码为32的足环是由中国火车头信鸽协会下发的，代码为33的足环是由中国石油信鸽协会下发的" — [chinaxinge baike 1003](https://news.chinaxinge.com/baike/view.asp?id=1003&sjm=a8iovyyknkh8).
- The earlier trial regulation 《中国信鸽协会信鸽足环管理办法(试行)》 (dated 2013-04-09, effective 2013-07-01) requires CHN (3 letters), the 4-digit year, and a number whose first 2 digits are the provincial code: "除年份、代码外，全国统一足环应为7位数字" — [chinaxinge news 82047](https://news.chinaxinge.com/shownews.asp?id=82047&sjm=40194c7d34e52b1e). The implementing notice (社体协会字(2013)72号, 2013-08-01) concerns the **2014** national rings, which also introduced QR-code anti-counterfeiting — [chinaxinge baike 1003](https://news.chinaxinge.com/baike/view.asp?id=1003&sjm=a8iovyyknkh8).
- **Older format (flag, before 2014):** "足环的标示上由四位数年份+二位数代码+六位数足环号组成，例如标示为2011.10.123456" (10 = Jiangsu) — [chinaxinge baike 1003](https://news.chinaxinge.com/baike/view.asp?id=1003&sjm=a8iovyyknkh8). Real 2012 rings cited in news items on the same page: "环号2012-19-080902" (Guangdong 潮南公棚) and "2012-26-193362 灰" (Shaanxi 大秦秋棚) — [same source](https://news.chinaxinge.com/baike/view.asp?id=1003&sjm=a8iovyyknkh8).
- Forum examples of how fanciers write it: "CHN2012-24-000001" (24 = Yunnan; 6-digit era) and "信鸽2011-01-123456" — [ask.chinaxinge 285939](https://ask.chinaxinge.com/htmlmother/htmlaskshow.aspx?qid=285939). "CHN2014-01-607196" — [loing-ma forum, via search snippet](https://www.loing-ma.com/viewtopic.php?f=65&t=11298). A 7-digit-era user comment: "2019-23-0228879的鸽子" — [chinaxinge baike 7607 comments](http://news.chinaxinge.com/baike/view.asp?id=7607&sjm=y1e0tamt3l7k).
- **Real current examples (2026, PIPR):** "CHN 26-15-0443185" (team "ANHUA 安华鸽业-王志安"), "CHN 26-10-0379111", "CHN 26-24-0918006", "CHN 26-02-0330733", "CHN 26-01-1126801". 850 of 854 CHN rings match `CHN YY-PP-NNNNNNN`. Province codes seen: 01 (232 rings), 03 (143), 09 (76), 15 (56), 22, 26, 19, 12, 10, 02, 27, 13, ... and 33 (1) — [PIPR participants](https://www.pattayaoneloftrace.com/en/participant). Four CHN rings in that list have only 6 serial digits ("CHN 26-19-012554", "CHN 26-26-000251"); these are probably data-entry errors or a special series (unverified).
- Western and lost-bird variants seen: "CHN-2023- -1254412" (US lost-pigeon board, user entry) — [homingpigeon.com, via search snippet](https://www.homingpigeon.com/lbdisplay.php?pg=6); "2019-32 CHN" (year first, CHN last) — [pigeons.biz thread, via search snippet](https://www.pigeons.biz/threads/can-someone-explain-pigeon-rings-to-me.71829/). Pigero (pigeon software) lists China formats "CHN 99-99 … CHN 99-9999999" — [Pigero help, via search snippet; the page itself failed DNS](https://help.pigero.com/docs/en/varia/supported-combinations).
- Foreign rings (including Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan) racing in mainland China must be registered with CXA under 中鸽协字2017年10号文 (Art. 21). One-loft (公棚) entries "足环代码不受区域限制", meaning any province code can appear in any loft — [chinaxinge baike 7607](http://news.chinaxinge.com/baike/view.asp?id=7607&sjm=y1e0tamt3l7k).
- The 2026 national ring order was 3,278万 (32.78 million) rings (headline "足环发行量再创新高：2026年征订3278万枚") — [chinaxinge news 82047 sidebar](https://news.chinaxinge.com/shownews.asp?id=82047&sjm=40194c7d34e52b1e).

### Inferences
- Normalisation regex (current): `^(?:CHN|CN|CHINA)?[\s\-.]*((?:19|20)?\d{2})[\s\-.]+(\d{2})[\s\-.]+(\d{6,7})$` → canonical `CHN-YYYY-PP-NNNNNNN`. The middle 2-digit block (00–33) is the province/sector code. It is not a club code or month.
- 6-digit serial ⇒ almost certainly a ring from 2013 or earlier. 7-digit serial ⇒ 2014 or later. The 2013/2014 cut-over is inferred from the 2013 trial regulation and the 2014 ring notice, not stated as a single date.
- Mainland 2-digit codes have no Chinese characters in the number. Romanisation is only needed to name the province (e.g. 22 = Sichuan).

### Gaps
- No photo of a current ring was found, so the exact printed order and separators (e.g. "CHN 2026 01 1234567" on one line vs two lines) are **unverified**. The regulation lists the elements, not their layout.
- The regulation's publication date (the revised version) was not visible. The page says only "创建时间：2018-2-28".
- An older 2001 code table was mentioned as possibly different; I did not find it.
- PIPA (pipa.be) listings of Chinese-ringed birds were not found, so I could not verify how PIPA itself writes CHN rings.

## 2. Taiwan (TW / TWN / TAIWAN / CTRPA ...; "Chinese Taipei")

### Takeaway
Taiwan has **no single national ring**. Each association or federation issues its own (CTRPA, KLPR, TPA, UN, DTRPA, ...). The typical ring is **PREFIX + 4-digit year + 6-digit serial** (e.g. "C.T.R.P.A 2027 311605", "TAIWAN 2026 302203", "TW2026 777984"). Club race rings are sold **per season**, and the year on the ring is the **season** year, which can be the next calendar year. Results pages write "25冬627958" (YY + season character + serial) and use ROC (民國) years for race names.

### Cited Findings
- Lost-bird reports (Taiwan, 2024–2026) quote ring texts verbatim: "TW2024888863", "TAIWAN 2015 582554", "Tw2026 777963", "TW2027 555736", "JSL2026 207904", "291183 k.s2016", "TW2026/22269", "UN2010 編號：142186", "Taiwan 2025 300305", "296943 台灣2027", "TPA 2025 #868292", "C.T.R.P.A 2027 311605", "C.T.R.P.A 2026 835352", "腳環[TWN2026] 777295", "002309 B.H 2026", "Taiwan 2007 7775617" (7 digits), "台灣2022 255130", "CTRPA 2027 701038", "KLPR 流水號 765789", "DTRPA2027 335556", "TAIWAN 2022 831160", "TPA 2026 756989" — [cb-pigeon 拾獲賽鴿](https://www.cb-pigeon.com/pick-up).
- Season ring sales, TPIUL (台灣大眾國際聯合鴿舍): "116春季售環:提環日115/8/15-9/25", "115冬季售環 … 4/20-5/20", "115夏季售環114/12/01-115/1/31". Years are ROC (115 = 2026, 116 = 2027) — [TPIUL](http://www.pigeonnet.com.tw/tpiul/).
- Prize listings on the same page write rings as YY + season + serial: "25冬627958灰(母)榮獲大北區五關綜合4位", "24春302841斑(母)", "21冬702194", "19春386169", "16冬223797", "15春6795797" (7 digits) — [TPIUL](http://www.pigeonnet.com.tw/tpiul/).
- Race result tables (e.g. "115夏季正一關比賽成績") show a short **環號** like "90" next to the loft number "1257", i.e. a per-race or per-loft number, not the full ring — [prxg results, via search snippet](http://www.prxg.org/tree/newprxg/results.php).
- PIPR 2026 (country "TW"): "TW 26-059966", "TW 26-059967", "CTRPA 26-086323", "KLPR 26-705304", all `PREFIX YY-NNNNNN` (6 digits). A Chinese (CN) team also entered CTRPA-ringed birds ("CTRPA 26-086317") — [PIPR participants](https://www.pattayaoneloftrace.com/en/participant).
- Older forum list of Taiwanese ring prefixes (2012, low reliability): CTRPA(CRPA) 中华民国赛鸽协会; TAIWAN / 台鴿 台湾省信鸽协会; TFPF (formerly CFPF) / TOIF 台湾海翔国际联盟; KLPR 高雄区信鸽协会; RPATC 台南县赛鸽协会; UN 桃源联合分会; ROC, THPA, C.R.P.A, R.O.N (屏东飞鸽协会), TLPR, NLPR, B.X; also "CHINA 中国信鸽总会" — [ask.chinaxinge 285939](https://ask.chinaxinge.com/htmlmother/htmlaskshow.aspx?qid=285939).
- A Baidu health article claims "中国台湾省为TPE" as the country code — [Baidu, via search snippet](https://health.baidu.com/m/detail/ar_7176455904753286668). The same page is internally contradictory (see the search summary), so treat this as **unverified**. I found no ring actually printed "TPE".

### Inferences
- The year on Taiwanese rings follows the racing season. Rings for the spring 2027 (116春) season go on sale in August 2026, which explains "TW2027 …" and "C.T.R.P.A 2027 …" already being found in 2026. Software should **not** reject a ring year of current year + 1 for TW.
- Serial is mostly 6 digits; 7 digits occur (2007, 2015). Prefix variants include dotted forms (C.T.R.P.A), Chinese (台灣) and the prefix after the number ("296943 台灣2027", "002309 B.H 2026"), so the parser should allow the prefix on either side.
- Suggested canonical form: `TW-<assoc>-YYYY-NNNNNN` (e.g. `TW-CTRPA-2027-311605`), keeping the association because serials are only unique within an association and season.

### Gaps
- No official CTRPA (中華民國賽鴿協會) ring specification was found. Whether the season (春/夏/秋/冬) is printed on the ring or only used in listings is **unverified**.
- Meaning of several prefixes (JSL, DTRPA, B.H, K.S, NHRPS, FM) is unverified.

## 3. Japan (JPN / JAPAN / NIPPON)

### Takeaway
There are two bodies with two formats. **Japan Racing Pigeon Association (日本鳩レース協会, JRPA):** `JPN 09 AB 12345` = JPN + 2-digit year + 2-letter code + 5-digit serial (Japanese lists write it "09KA35132"). **Japan Pigeon Federation (日本伝書鳩協会):** `NIPPON 2009 123456` = NIPPON + 4-digit year + 6-digit serial.

### Cited Findings
- Niigata Prefecture guidance: there are two organisations, 日本鳩レース協会 and 日本伝書鳩協会. Rings starting with "JAPAN" or "JPN" belong to 日本鳩レース協会, "例）JPN 09 AB 12345". Rings starting with "NIPPON" belong to 日本伝書鳩協会, "例）NIPPON 2009 123456" — [Niigata Pref.](https://www.pref.niigata.lg.jp/sec/kankyotaisaku/ashiwaaicho.html). (These are illustrative examples, not real birds.)
- The JRPA 2010 best-flyer award document lists pigeon numbers such as "09KA35132" and "09MM00050", with affiliations like 埼玉地区連盟 埼玉北辰連合会 and 東京中地区連盟 新日本連合会 — [JRPA best_flyer 2010 PDF, via search snippet](https://jrpa.or.jp/app/webroot/prize/best_flyer/2010.pdf). I could not extract the PDF text myself (Japanese CID fonts).
- PIPR 2026, Japanese entries (75 rings): all `JPN YY-LL-NNNNN`, e.g. "JPN 26-EM-00552", "JPN 26-EM-00555". Letter codes seen: HF (25), ET (19), XB, MB, LK (9 each), EM (4) — [PIPR participants](https://www.pattayaoneloftrace.com/en/participant).
- A 2012 Chinese forum list names "NippON 日本传书鸽协会" and "Japan 日本赛鸽协会" — [ask.chinaxinge 285939](https://ask.chinaxinge.com/htmlmother/htmlaskshow.aspx?qid=285939).

### Inferences
- JRPA regex: `^(?:JPN|JAPAN|JP|JA)?[\s\-]*(\d{2})[\s\-]*([A-Z]{2})[\s\-]*(\d{5})$`. NIPPON regex: `^NIPPON[\s\-]*(\d{4})[\s\-]*(\d{6})$`. The two-letter block in the JRPA ring is probably a regional or club code. That fits the 2010 document's pairing of the code with 連合会, but it is **unverified**.
- "JA" and "JP" as Japanese prefixes were not seen in any source. Accept them leniently, but they are not evidenced.

### Gaps
- No official JRPA table mapping the 2-letter codes to regions or unions. No 2026 photo of a JRPA ring. Whether 日本伝書鳩協会 still uses "NIPPON YYYY NNNNNN" in 2026 is unverified (the example uses 2009).

## 4. South Korea (KOR / KR)

### Takeaway
Very little data. One-loft entries write `KR 26-KRPC-0503` (country + year + club/association code + 4-digit serial) or `KR 26-0286`. No official Korean federation spec was found.

### Cited Findings
- PIPR 2026, Korean entries: "KR 26-KRPC-0503", "KR 26-KRPC-0512", "KR 26-KRPC-0701", "KR 26-KRPC-0704" (13 rings), plus "KR 26-0286", "KR 26-0288" — [PIPR participants](https://www.pattayaoneloftrace.com/en/participant).
- Searches in Korean (경주비둘기 족환 번호, KRPA) returned nothing relevant (no source).

### Inferences
- "KOR" is the ISO/IOC code and may appear on pedigrees, but no pigeon source showing "KOR" was found (**unverified**). Accept KOR/KR as synonyms.

### Gaps
- The Korean national body's name and its ring spec, the meaning of "KRPC", and Korean-script text on rings are all unverified.

## 5. Thailand (TH / THA / THAI)

### Takeaway
Thai rings in the 2026 PIPR list are written `TH <letter(s)> YY-NNNNN`, e.g. "TH P 26-…", "TH R 26-28203", "TH NKPT 26-21001". The letter is an issuing association or club code (meaning unverified). An older 2022 one-loft listing writes "THAI22-53111".

### Cited Findings
- PIPR 2026, Thai entries (573 of 592 rings in the form `TH A 99-99999`): letter codes P (278), R (157), C (70), H (58), M (44), plus "TH NKPT 26-21001" (15). Examples: "TH C 26-40810", "TH H 26-11008", "TH R 26-28203". Chinese teams also entered TH-ringed birds — [PIPR participants](https://www.pattayaoneloftrace.com/en/participant).
- A 2022 PIPR final-race report names "Rocket 111 THAI22-53111" — [oneloftracing.com, via search snippet](https://oneloftracing.com/pattaya-international-pigeon-race-final-race/).
- Thailand Masters 2025/26 rule: "Each bird must have a valid 2025 official country ring" (no format given) — [Amazing Wings](https://amazing-wings.com/en/?p=6149).

### Inferences
- Regex: `^(?:TH|THA|THAI)[\s\-]*([A-Z]{1,4})?[\s\-]*(\d{2})[\s\-]+(\d{5})$`. The 5-digit serial seems to be specific to the letter series.

### Gaps
- No Thai association (e.g. Racing Pigeon International Association / Thai federation) document explaining the letters P/R/C/H/M/NKPT. Thai-script text on rings is unknown. "THA" was not seen in any pigeon source.

## 6. Philippines (PHA / PH / PHIL / FPR / PUF ...)

### Takeaway
There are several federations. The **Philippine Homing Association (PHA)** ring is written "PHA 22A284639" (YY + letter + 6 digits). One-loft lists show "PHA 26-5015967", "PHA 26-A093141" or "PHA 26-393151". Other bodies use their own prefixes: FPR, PUF NORTH, BBC MANILA, ASRPC, APR, VHPA, BFRPCI, CU, and PH + 3-letter club code.

### Cited Findings
- A Philippine fancier's race record: "2024 PHA 22A284639 NDR/OB", "2024 PHA 22A007979 ISR/OB", "2026 FPR 25-250106 SUM/YB" — [melbarcoma Jimdo](https://melbarcoma.jimdofree.com/previous-race-summary/).
- PIPR 2026, Philippine entries: "PHA 26-5015967", "PHA 26-2696101" (109 rings `PHA YY-NNNNNNN`), "PHA 26-393151" (6 digits), "PHA 26-A093141" (letter + 6), "PH 26-JKM-716010", "PH 26-MAL-…", "PH 26-NB-1021", "PUF NORTH 26-B254901", "BBC MANILA 26-C09029", "ASRPC 26-260043", "APR 26-2611901", "VHPA 26-0408", "BFRPCI 26-61221", "CU 26-CU-5009", "PHIL 26-…" — [PIPR participants](https://www.pattayaoneloftrace.com/en/participant).
- Forum mentions: "PHA2024 620803"; PHA patented an oblong ring and told clubs to stop using oblong bands from 2012 (old thread) — [pigeons.biz PHA thread, via search snippet](https://www.pigeons.biz/threads/pha-philippine-homing-association.51655/).
- PHA leg bands are in active use (2026 police seizure of racing pigeons with "Philippine Homing Association (PHA) leg band numbers") — [Manila Bulletin 2026-03-25, via search snippet](https://mb.com.ph/2026/03/25/cops-net-two-racing-pigeon-lambateros-in-batangas-operation).

### Inferences
- The PHA letter after the year (22**A**284639) seems to be part of the ring. PIPR's "PHA 26-5015967" (7 digits, no letter) may be a different PHA series or the letter written as a digit. **Unverified**: keep the letter if present. Regex: `^PHA[\s\-]*(\d{2})[\s\-]*([A-Z])?(\d{6,7})$`.
- In the race record, the **race** year (2024) is listed before the ring, and the ring's own year is 22. Do not confuse the two.

### Gaps
- No official PHA specification. The meaning of the letter, and of FPR, PUF, BBC and the others, is unverified. Whether "PHI"/"PHL" is ever used was not seen.

## 8. Indonesia (INA / ID / IDN)

### Takeaway
Sparse. One-loft entries write "ID 26-074318" (6 digits) or club-prefixed "POMP 26-WSK-6201" and "LLB 26-IDN-6338". No national spec was found. Most Indonesian "merpati balap" is sprint racing, where "ring" can mean the arena.

### Cited Findings
- PIPR 2026, Indonesian entries: "ID 26-074318", "ID 26-114369", "ID 26-018300", "POMP 26-WSK-6201" (12 rings), "LLB 26-IDN-6338" — [PIPR participants](https://www.pattayaoneloftrace.com/en/participant).
- In Indonesian sprint racing, "ring" refers to the landing arena, not the leg band ("siapa yang cepat menangkap dan tidak terjatuh dari ring") — [Mongabay ID, via search snippet](https://www.mongabay.co.id/2019/11/24/adu-cepat-burung-merpati-makin-diminati-para-penghobi/).

### Gaps
- "INA" (the IOC code) on pigeon rings was not seen. The meaning of POMP, WSK and LLB is unverified.

## 9. Vietnam (VN)

### Takeaway
No national standard was found. Clubs set their own ring rules. One-loft entries write "VN 26-TML-666008".

### Cited Findings
- PIPR 2026, Vietnamese entries: "VN 26-TML-666008", "VN 26-TML-666015", "VN 26-TML-666047" (`VN YY-AAA-NNNNNN`) — [PIPR participants](https://www.pattayaoneloftrace.com/en/participant).

## 11. How Western / one-loft / auction sites write Asian rings, and the ambiguities

### Takeaway
International one-loft lists (PIPR) normalise every Asian ring to `PREFIX YY-…`, using the **issuing body's prefix**, which is not always the owner's country. Many Asian prefixes are association acronyms (CTRPA, KLPR, PHA, HKPA, POMP), not ISO codes, and several collide with other countries or with club codes.

### Cited Findings
- PIPR stores the owner's country (e.g. "CN") separately from the ring prefix. Chinese teams entered birds ringed NL, AU, BE, DV, RO, PL, PRT, TH, CTRPA and CHN — [PIPR participants](https://www.pattayaoneloftrace.com/en/participant).
- In Malta's PIPR entries "MALTA 26-04-4205", the shape `PREFIX YY-NN-NNNN` resembles the Chinese `CHN YY-PP-NNNNNNN` but has a 4-digit serial — [PIPR participants](https://www.pattayaoneloftrace.com/en/participant).
- In UK race software, a ring needs a country/union code and a 2-digit year — [NFC verification PDF, via search snippet](https://nationalflyingclub.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/Verification.pdf).

### Inferences: ambiguity list for the parser
- **Owner country ≠ ring country**: never infer the ring prefix from the owner's nationality (a Chinese owner can hold NL, TH or CTRPA rings).
- **CHN `YY-PP-NNNNNNN`**: the middle 2 digits are a province code. Do not treat them as a club number (as in Polish "PL 26-0221-3103") or a year. A 7-digit serial after a 2-digit code is a strong CHN signal. A bare "2019-23-0228879" with no prefix is very likely Chinese.
- **TW**: the ring year may be current + 1 (season rings). ROC years (114/115/116) appear in Taiwanese listings, but not on the rings seen. "台灣" and "TAIWAN" can come after the number. "CHINA" was listed in a 2012 forum as a Taiwanese prefix (中国信鸽总会) and conflicts with mainland "CHINA" (**unverified**, low reliability).
- **TPE** (sports code for Chinese Taipei): claimed by one unreliable page, never seen on a ring.
- **JPN**: the 2-letter codes (ET, LK, MB, HF, XB, EM, KA, MM) collide with ISO country codes (ET = Ethiopia, LK = Sri Lanka, MM = Myanmar). Inside `JPN YY-LL-NNNNN` they are not countries.
- **"MAL"**: a Philippine club code ("PH 26-MAL-…"), possibly Malaysia, and "MALTA". **"PHIL"** vs **"PHA"** vs **"PH"**: all Philippine.
- **"UN"** (Taiwan 桃源聯合分會) is not a country code.
- **"ID"**: Indonesia, but also a generic word. **"IDN"** appears as a club code inside an LLB ring.
- **"KR"/"KRPC"**: KRPC is a club code inside a KR ring.
- **Year position**: Philippine race records put the race year before the ring ("2024 PHA 22A284639"). Taiwanese prize lists put YY + season before the serial ("25冬627958").

### Gaps
- I could not confirm how **PIPA** (pipa.be) or other European auction houses write Chinese or Taiwanese rings on pedigrees (no PIPA pages with Asian rings found). Pigero's format list was only seen as a snippet (DNS failure).
